ABOUT US

Pax & Choco Publishing

We make puzzle books for players. People who grew up blowing into cartridges, saving quarters for the arcade and staying up far too late for one more level. Or who wish they had.

We are a tiny independent studio. Every puzzle in our books is written, tested and checked by hand, then checked again by a solver to make sure it has exactly one answer. If a puzzle is unfair, we consider that our bug, not yours.

Our first book, The Ultimate Video Game Puzzle Book, hides a secret level across all of its pages. Finding it is optional. Finding it is also the best part.

Pax and Choco are our dogs. They did not help with the puzzles. They helped with everything else.
